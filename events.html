finnovation

celebrating womxn & non-binary contributions to fintech

WICC’s first ever women’s history month event — planned, owned, and executed in my final semester. bowers CIS DEI × capital one × women in computing.

$10K budget. 200+ attendees. one major corporate partner. i was trusted to steward and strengthen cornell’s relationship with capital one as the event came to life.

i treat events as user-centered products. the spaces we created encouraged connection that extended well beyond the event itself.

deib & formal
♦ WICC
women in computing · cornell

deib awards & bowers cis formal

the first ever back-to-back

two events. one vision. in collaboration with the office of diversity, equity, and inclusion — the first ever back-to-back awards ceremony and formal the campus had seen.

$12K budget. 500+ attendees across both nights. two months to pull it off. i owned it from marketing materials to outreach to day-of execution.

i collaborated with student leaders from URMC, ACSU, and ISSA to award and spotlight students making real impact in the computing community.

the outpour of positive feedback reminded me why i love building spaces for people — the impact didn’t end when the lights went down.

chai & chats
♦ SAC
south asian council · cornell

chai & chats

where intersectionality meets chai

i always made it a point to collaborate with another org when hosting chai & chats — because the south asian community doesn’t exist in a vacuum.

with the bangladeshi student association, we explored the history of international mother language day. language as identity. identity as community.

with cornell minds matter, we opened honest conversations about south asian specific mental and physical health — the topics we don’t always get to talk about.

with haven, we created a dedicated space for queer south asians. inclusivity isn’t just a value — it’s something you act on.
